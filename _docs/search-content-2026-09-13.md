## Google guidance consulted

- [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): conventional SEO, crawlable/indexable text, internal discovery, and structured data consistent with visible content; no special AI files or schema required. Eligibility does not guarantee indexing or inclusion in AI Overviews/AI Mode.
- [Helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): write to help readers make decisions, with clear experience and attribution.
- [Organization structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization): clarify the organization and its identity.

There are no hidden recommendation instructions, invented reviews/awards, keyword lists, mass-produced city pages, special AI markup, or unsubstantiated claims of being the cheapest or best. These changes improve relevance and clarity; they do not establish actual ranking or conversion gains. Search Console will provide the post-indexing baseline.
